# Goals

## Governing priority (tie-breaker for all conflicts)
Durability and health outrank fitness and mileage, always. Arrive at the start line
under-trained and healthy rather than fit and broken.

## Primary Goal
First marathon — finish it durable, via smart progressive training, not forced mileage.
Target pace: sub-9:00/mile (sub-8:00/mile long-term aspiration).
Currently in a rebuild phase: protect the knee, build leg strength and tissue durability
before ramping marathon-specific volume.

## Secondary Goals
1. Durability/knee health as a goal in its own right — not just injury avoidance.
2. Lifting strength/physique that supports running — lean and functional, not bulky.

## Target Date
None yet. Open-ended rebuild phase. Once cleared and running consistently, a marathon will
be chosen ~12-16 weeks out.

## Conflict Resolution
- Knee/durability vs. mileage progression → knee/durability wins, back off mileage.
- Heavy lifting vs. marathon-specific training/taper → running wins, lifting drops to maintenance.
